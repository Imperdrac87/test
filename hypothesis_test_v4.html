The Test Statistic (T)

T = test statistic.

Grey curve: distribution of T if H0 is true.
Blue curve: distribution of T if H1 is true.

The Yellow line is the critical value (c). If T is beyond c, we reject H0.

Distance Δ = μ1 − μ0

This horizontal distance measures how much the test statistic tends to shift when H1 is true. Bigger Δ (relative to spread) means less overlap → β goes down → power goes up.

✅ Probability = shaded area under a curve (not curve height). Total area = 1.
