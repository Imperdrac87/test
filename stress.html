Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Click on each muscle group for guided relaxation instructions

🧠 Face & Head

Tense your facial muscles, scrunch your forehead, close your eyes tightly. Hold for 5 seconds, then release and feel the relaxation.

🤷 Shoulders & Neck

Raise your shoulders up to your ears, tense your neck muscles. Hold for 5 seconds, then let them drop and relax completely.

💪 Arms & Hands

Make fists and tense your arm muscles from hands to shoulders. Hold tightly for 5 seconds, then release and let your arms go limp.

🫁 Chest & Back

Take a deep breath and tense your chest and back muscles. Hold for 5 seconds, then exhale and let all tension melt away.

🤰 Stomach

Tighten your abdominal muscles as if preparing for a punch. Hold for 5 seconds, then release and breathe naturally.

🦵 Legs & Feet

Tense your leg muscles from thighs to toes, point your feet. Hold for 5 seconds, then release and feel the heaviness of relaxation.
