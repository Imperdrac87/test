IDE Interface & Controls

The Workspace

Source (Top Left) Your mission briefing. Also serves as the **Data Viewer**. Click a blue dataframe in Environment to open a new tab here.
Environment (Top Right) Displays active objects. Click on blue dataframes to view their contents.
Console (Bottom Left) The command line. Type your R code here and press ENTER to execute.
Viewer (Bottom Right) Contains your Score, Timer, and the Control Panel (Tools & Menu).

Control Panel Tools

HINT Progressive text clues. Small point cost, but keeps your streak alive.
STR() Inspects the "Structure" of the answer. Reveals syntax patterns without solving it.
REVEAL Auto-types the correct answer. Heavy penalty (-400 pts). Use as a last resort.
ABORT / MENU Immediately exit the current session and return to the main menu.
