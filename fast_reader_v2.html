The Scholar's Manual

FOCUS: The RSVP Method

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) is the core engine. By displaying words one-at-a-time at high speed, it eliminates Subvocalization (speaking in your head) and eye movement. Focus strictly on the Red Pivot character to absorb information directly into your short-term memory.

Bionic Snapshots

Bionic Reading bolds the initial parts of words. This acts as a Visual Fixation Point. Instead of your eye scanning the whole word, your brain takes a "snapshot" of the bolded section and automatically identifies the rest. This significantly reduces cognitive load during long PhD-level papers.

The Buzan Techniques

The Visual Pacer: Inspired by Tony Buzan's pointer technique. In Stream mode, the "Pacer" adds a moving underline. This simulates a finger leading the eye, maintaining a constant forward rhythm and preventing Regression (the habit of re-reading previous lines).

Soft Focus Training: This mode fades the text margins. It trains your Peripheral Vision to capture the overall structure and context while your central focus stays on the "center-line" of information, maximizing "eye-brain" intake.

Quick Controls:

  • Space: Play / Stop Reading
  • J / L: Skip forward/back 10 words
  • Arrows Up/Down: Speed Control
