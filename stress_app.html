🧠 Thought Reframing Workspace

Examine your thoughts without judgment. This is CBT-inspired but self-directed—no manipulation, no forced optimism. You choose what feels most honest.

Step 1: Evidence Supporting This Thought

What facts or experiences support this thought? Be honest and specific.

Step 2: Evidence Against This Thought

What facts or experiences contradict this thought? Look for counter-examples.

Step 3: Alternative Interpretations

What other ways could you view this situation? Consider different perspectives.

Step 4: What Feels Most Honest?

Based on your examination, what perspective feels most balanced and truthful?

Body Scan Exercise

Take a few minutes to notice physical sensations without judgment.

Instructions

  1. Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down
  2. Close your eyes or soften your gaze
  3. Start at the top of your head and slowly move your attention down
  4. Notice any sensations: tension, warmth, coolness, tingling
  5. Don't try to change anything, just observe
  6. If your mind wanders, gently return to the body part you were focusing on
